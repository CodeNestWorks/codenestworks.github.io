QR CODE GENERATOR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

QR CODE GENERATOR is developed and published by CodeNest. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

## 2. Information Collection

QR CODE GENERATOR does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The app does not intentionally collect or transmit QR code content, entered URLs, or customized theme preferences to the developer or an external server.

## 3. Local Data Storage

The app processes QR code generation directly on your device.

Generated QR code images are saved in the application's local storage.

Theme preferences are saved locally on your device.

The app does not operate its own cloud storage service.

Android device backup settings may include eligible application data in device backups, depending on your device and account settings.

## 4. Internet and External Websites

The app generates QR codes from user-provided HTTP or HTTPS addresses. It does not itself browse to those websites or transmit the entered address to a developer-operated server.

If you scan or use a generated QR code with another application, that application or the destination website may process information under its own privacy policy.

## 5. Advertising and Analytics

The supplied application source and build configuration do not show advertising or analytics integrations.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## 7. Security

The app stores its generated images and preferences in application-specific device storage. Users should protect their devices and review their Android backup settings.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
